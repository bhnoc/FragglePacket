Test HTTPS connectivity with stage-by-stage analysis (MTU blackhole detection)

Usage: fraggle-packet https [OPTIONS] <TARGET>

Arguments:
  <TARGET>  Target hostname (e.g., google.com, github.com)

Options:
  -T, --timeout <TIMEOUT>  Timeout in seconds [default: 10]
  -d, --diagnose           Show diagnosis and recommendations
  -h, --help               Print help
