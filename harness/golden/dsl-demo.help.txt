Print a hexdump of a packet described by our DSL (demo helper)

Usage: fraggle-packet dsl-demo [OPTIONS]

Options:
  -d, --dst <DST>    Destination IPv4 [default: 1.1.1.1]
  -p, --port <PORT>  Destination port [default: 443]
      --size <SIZE>  Payload size in bytes [default: 32]
  -h, --help         Print help
